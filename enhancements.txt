Fixed income low risk
Fixed Deposits (FDs): Lock money in a bank at a fixed interest rate for a specific tenure.
Recurring Deposits (RDs): Invest small, fixed amounts every month into a bank or post office.
Government Bonds & Securities: Loans given to the government in exchange for regular interest and return of principal.
Public Provident Fund (PPF): Government-backed, long-term savings scheme with tax benefits.
Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS): Post office or bank accounts for retirees offering guaranteed regular income.
Corporate Bonds: Loans provided to private companies in exchange for fixed coupon payments

market linked medium-high risk
Stocks / Equities: Buying shares that represent direct ownership in a publicly traded company.
Mutual Funds: Professionally managed pools of money that invest in diversified stocks, bonds, or both.
ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds): Index funds that track specific markets and can be traded on stock exchanges like regular shares.
ULIPs (Unit-Linked Insurance Plans): Hybrid financial products providing both life insurance and market-linked investment returns.
National Pension System (NPS): Government-sponsored pension scheme that invests in a mix of equity and debt for retirement.

Physical assets
Real Estate: Purchasing land, residential, or commercial properties for capital appreciation and rental income.
REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts): Buying shares of companies that own/operate commercial real estate, allowing you to earn rental income without buying a building yourself.
Gold & Precious Metals: Investing in physical gold, sovereign gold bonds, or gold ETFs to hedge against inflation.
Cryptocurrencies: Digital or virtual currencies (e.g., Bitcoin) secured by cryptography.
Private Equity / Hedge Funds: Pooled investment vehicles that are not publicly traded, typically available to high-net-worth individuals
